# Inspiration from Kenya's device stores

How Steff Store borrows what works from the leading Kenyan electronics shops — and where it
deliberately does better.

> Method note: the build environment's network policy blocked direct visits to the stores (and to
> steffcloud.co.ke), so these notes come from search results and from the stores' well-known public
> patterns. Brand values were taken from the Steff Cloud source code and the Steff Cloud brand kit.

## Stores reviewed

| Store | Known for | What we took |
|---|---|---|
| Jumia Kenya | Huge catalogue, filters, flash sales with % badges, pickup stations | Filter sidebar (brand, budget, condition, stock, deals), `−10%` deal badges, a pickup option at checkout |
| Phone Place Kenya | Deep phone range, WhatsApp ordering everywhere | **Order on WhatsApp** on every product, cart and order confirmation |
| Avechi | Category-first navigation, deal sections | Persistent category bar, a deals page |
| Hotpoint Appliances | Big brands, showrooms, Easy Pay financing | **Lipa Mdogo Mdogo** page with a calculator, a "Visit the shop" section with a click-to-load map |
| Kilimall | Budget shoppers | "Shop by budget" chips (under 15K → over 200K) |
| Patabay, Smart Devices Kenya, Shopit, Zuricart, Overtech | Same-day Nairobi delivery, countrywide in 1–3 days, "genuine + warranty" messaging | Delivery zones for all 47 counties, a per-county delivery estimate on every product, a promise list |
| Lipa Mdogo Mdogo specialists | Deposit plus monthly M-Pesa payments | Deposit and monthly estimate shown under every price over KES 10K |

## Where Steff Store goes further

1. **Whitespace and clarity.** Competitors pack the homepage. We show fewer, clearer choices on an 8-point grid.
2. **Honest deals.** Each deal shows the old price and counts down to a real end date (`dealsEndAt`). No timers that reset at midnight.
3. **No fake social proof.** No invented ratings, review counts or "1,000 sold".
4. **Ex-UK transparency.** Every ex-UK device is labelled, graded and tested, with its own warranty.
5. **Decision tools.** Compare up to four devices, laptops grouped by use case (three per group), "Ask a question" on WhatsApp.
6. **Kenya-first checkout.** M-Pesa STK Push, pay on delivery in Nakuru, Lipa Mdogo Mdogo, bank transfer for organisations.
7. **Built properly.** 158 pre-rendered pages, product and breadcrumb structured data, a sitemap, dark mode, accessible keyboard navigation, consent-gated analytics and a Data Protection Act-aligned privacy policy.
